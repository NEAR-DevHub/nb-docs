## Setting Up Ledger with NEAR

1. Download and install [Ledger Live](https://www.ledger.com/ledger-live) on your computer.
2. Connect your Ledger to your computer via USB.
3. Open the **Ledger Live** application and ensure your device firmware is up to date.
4. Install the **NEAR app** on your Ledger from the *App Catalog* in Ledger Live.
5. Open the NEAR app on your Ledger device (the screen should display *Application is ready*).

![NEAR app installed in Ledger Live](/assets/docs/ledger/trezu-ledger-near-app.png)
*NEAR app (v2.4.3) installed in Ledger Live*

Your wallet is now backed by your Ledger. All transaction signatures will require physical confirmation on the device.

---

## 1. Logging In

1. Click **Connect Wallet** in the top-right corner.
2. Select your wallet (connected to your Ledger).
3. If using Ledger, your device will prompt you to **confirm the connection** — approve it on the device.
4. Select the account you want to use and **approve the selection on your Ledger device**.
5. Enter your **Account ID**. If the account does not exist, Trezu will allow you to create it.
6. You will be redirected to your dashboard once authenticated.

## 2. Creating a Treasury

### Step 1 – Start a New Treasury

1. From the dashboard, click **Create Treasury**.
2. Enter a **Treasury Name** (e.g., `My DAO Treasury`).
3. Click **Next**.

![Start a new treasury](/assets/docs/ledger/create-step-1.png)

### Step 2 – Add Members and Assign Roles

In this example, we will set up a treasury with **4 members**:

| Member          | Role         |
| --------------- | ------------ |
| Alice (you)     | Governance   |
| Bob             | Governance   |
| Carol           | Finance      |
| Dave            | Requestor    |

1. In the **Members** screen, add each member's wallet address.
2. Assign [roles](/governance/members-and-roles) to each member:
   - Set **Alice** and **Bob** as **Governance**.
   - Set **Carol** as **Finance**.
   - Set **Dave** as **Requestor**.
3. Configure the **Voting Thresholds**:
   - **Governance threshold**: `1` (one Governance member can approve configuration changes).
   - **Finance threshold**: `1` (one Finance member can approve financial requests).
4. Click **Create Treasury**.

![Adding members during treasury creation](/assets/docs/ledger/create-step-2.png)
*Adding 4 members with their respective roles*

5. Your Ledger will prompt you to **confirm the transaction** — approve it on the device.
6. Wait for the transaction to be confirmed on-chain. Once done, you will see your new treasury dashboard.

![Treasury created successfully](/assets/docs/ledger/create-step-3.png)

<Info>
  Each member will need to connect with their own wallet to participate in the treasury. Make sure all members have access to their wallets before proceeding.
</Info>

## 3. Adding a New Governance Member via Proposal

Now that the treasury is set up, let's walk through adding a **third Governance member** (Eve) by creating and voting on a proposal.

### Step 1 – Create the Proposal

Only members with the **Governance** role can propose membership changes.

1. Log in with a **Governance** account (e.g., Alice).
2. Navigate to the **Members** section in the left sidebar.
3. Click **Add Member**.
4. Enter **Eve's wallet address**.
5. Assign the **Governance** role to Eve.
6. Click **Submit Proposal**.
7. Your Ledger will prompt you to **sign the transaction** — approve it on the device.
8. The proposal is now created and visible in the **Pending Requests** section.

### Step 2 – Vote on the Proposal

Since the Governance voting threshold is set to **1**, only one Governance member needs to approve the proposal.

1. Still logged in as **Alice**, go to Requests and open the Pending Requests tab.
2. Find the proposal to add Eve.
3. Click **Approve**.
4. Your Ledger will prompt you to **confirm the vote** — approve it on the device.

*Alice approving the proposal to add Eve*

Once Alice approves, the proposal reaches the required threshold and is **automatically executed**, adding Eve as a new Governance member.
