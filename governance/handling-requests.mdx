## Voting on a Request[​](#voting-on-a-request "Direct link to Voting on a Request")

Click on a request to review its details, including the type of request, the proposed changes, and any comments added by the requester. After reviewing the request, you can cast your vote to either approve or reject it.

![Request Details](/assets/docs/governance/request-details.png)

<Warning>
  Only team members with the **[Governance](/governance/members-and-roles#governance) role** can review the details of the request and cast their vote to either **approve** or **reject** it.
</Warning>

***

## Filtering Payment Requests[​](#filtering-payment-requests "Direct link to Filtering Payment Requests")

You can filter payment requests by the following fields: `Request Type`, `Created Date`, `Recipient`, `Token`, `Requester`, `Approver`, `My Vote status`.

To filter payment requests click on the **`Filter`** icon:

![Request](/assets/docs/governance/request-filter.png)

Then simply add as many filters as needed:

![Request](/assets/docs/governance/filtered-requests.png)

<Check>
  To see only governance requests, filter by `Request Type` -> `Change Policy`
</Check>
