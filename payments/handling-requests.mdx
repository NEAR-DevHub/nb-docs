description: "Manage payment requests in your treasury"
---

Once a payment request is created—either for a [single payment](/payments/single-payment) or to pay [multiple recipients at once](/payments/bulk-payment)—it is listed as a **`Pending`** request in the **`Requests`** section.

![Pending payment requests](/assets/docs/payments/pending-requests.webp)

Navigate to the **`Requests`** section in the left sidebar to see all pending payment requests. You can filter and sort requests to find the one you want to review and vote on.

<Tip>
  Only team members with the **[Finance](/governance/members-and-roles#finance) role** can review request details and cast a vote to either **approve** or **reject**.
</Tip>

---

## Voting on a Request

Click on a request to review its details, including the recipient, the amount, the token, and any comments added by the requester. After reviewing the request, you can cast your vote to either approve or reject it.

![Payment request details](/assets/docs/payments/request-details.webp)

<Warning>
  Only team members with the **[Finance](/governance/members-and-roles#finance) role** can review request details and cast a vote to either **approve** or **reject**.
</Warning>

<Info>
  If the treasury does not have enough funds to cover the payment request, you will need to top up the treasury with the required tokens before the request can be approved.
</Info>

---

## Receipts

Once a payment request is approved and executed, you can generate a PDF receipt to share with the recipient as proof of payment.

Select the payment for which you want to generate a receipt, then click **`Generate PDF Receipt`**.

![Generate PDF receipt option](/assets/docs/payments/generate-receipt.webp)

---

## Filtering Payment Requests

You can filter payment requests by the following fields: **`Request Type`**, **`Created Date`**, **`Recipient`**, **`Token`**, **`Requester`**, **`Approver`**, and **`My Vote Status`**.

To filter payment requests, click the **`Filter`** icon:

![Payment request filters](/assets/docs/payments/request-filter.webp)

Then add as many filters as needed:

![Filtered payment requests](/assets/docs/payments/filtered-requests.webp)

<Check>
  To see only payment requests, filter by **`Request Type`** → **`Payments`**.
</Check>
