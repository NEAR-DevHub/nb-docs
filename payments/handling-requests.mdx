---
title: "Handling Payment Requests"
description: "Manage payment requests in your treasury"
---

Once a payment request is created - either for a [single payment](./single-payment.mdx) or to pay [multiple recipients at one](./bulk-payment.mdx) - it will be listed as a `Pending` request in the `Requests` section.

![Request](/assets/docs/payments/pending-requests.png)

Navigate to the `Requests` section to see all pending payment requests. You can filter and sort the requests to find the one you want to review and vote on.

<Tip>
  Only team members with the **[Finance](/governance/members-and-roles#finance) role** can review the details of the request and cast their vote to either **approve** or **reject** it.
</Tip>

***

## Voting on a Request[​](#voting-on-a-request "Direct link to Voting on a Request")

Click on a request to review its details, including the recipient, the amount, the token, and any comments added by the requester. After reviewing the request, you can cast your vote to either approve or reject it.

![Request Details](/assets/docs/payments/request-details.png)

<Warning>
  Only team members with the **[Finance](/governance/members-and-roles#finance) role** can review the details of the request and cast their vote to either **approve** or **reject** it.
</Warning>

<Info>
  If the treasury does not have enough funds to cover the payment request, you will need to top-up the treasury with the required tokens before the request can be approved
</Info>

***

## Filtering Payment Requests[​](#filtering-payment-requests "Direct link to Filtering Payment Requests")

You can filter payment requests by the following fields: `Request Type`, `Created Date`, `Recipient`, `Token`, `Requester`, `Approver`, `My Vote status`.

To filter payment requests click on the **`Filter`** icon:

![Request](/assets/docs/payments/request-filter.png)

Then simply add as many filters as needed:

![Request](/assets/docs/payments/filtered-requests.png)

<Check>
  To see only payment requests, filter by `Request Type` -> `Payments`
</Check>
