If your team needs to [make a payment](/payments/single-payment) in a token that the treasury does not currently hold, or if you want to rebalance the treasury's portfolio, you can create a swap request to exchange one token for another.

![Asset swap overview](/assets/docs/swap/swap-landing.webp)

You can swap assets directly in NEAR Business, making it easy to manage your treasury's portfolio and access liquidity when needed.

---

## Creating a Swap Request

To swap assets, navigate to the **`Exchange`** section in the left sidebar. Select the assets you want to swap from and to, enter the amount, and review the details before confirming the request.

![Swap request review](/assets/docs/swap/swap-review.webp)

<Warning>
  Only team members with the [Requestor](/governance/members-and-roles#requestor) role can create swap requests.
</Warning>

### Swap Fee

NEAR Business charges a 0.7% fee on the amount being swapped. This fee is included in your quote and covers the costs of the underlying swap protocol—NEAR Intents—and the NEAR Business fee.

<Check>
  Swaps in NEAR Business are built on NEAR Intents, a platform for multi-chain asset swaps that provides competitive rates and low fees across different blockchains.
</Check>


---

## What's Next?

Swap requests remain open for **24 hours**. During this time, members with the [Finance](/governance/members-and-roles#finance) role can vote on them.

If the request does not receive enough votes—as determined by the treasury's [voting configuration](/governance/voting-settings)—within the 24-hour period, it will be automatically rejected.
