---
title: "What is a Treasury?"
description: "Treasuries can help teams to manage all their assets securely and privately"
---

A treasury lets teams manage crypto assets across multiple chains while keeping their financial data private. Only treasury members can view this information.

Under the hood, each treasury is a multisig wallet in which members have specific roles. Every action starts as a proposal. In the NEAR Business interface, proposals are shown as requests. Eligible members review and vote on them before execution.

<Note>
  NEAR Business is non-custodial and does not have access to your private keys or funds. Your team retains full control over your assets.
</Note>

---

## Confidential Treasuries

In NEAR Business, all **treasuries are confidential**. Unlike classic public treasuries, their financial data is **visible only to team members**.

| Feature                                        | Public Treasuries        | NEAR Business              |
|------------------------------------------------|--------------------------|----------------------------|
| Team members                                   | 🌐 Visible to anyone     | 🌐 Visible to anyone       |
| View the treasury funds                        | 🌐 Visible to anyone     | 🔒 Only the team           |
| View payment requests (current and historical) | 🌐 Visible to anyone     | 🔒 Only the team           |
| View swap requests (current and historical)    | 🌐 Visible to anyone     | 🔒 Only the team           |
| How transactions are handled                   | 🌐 Through public chains | 🔒 Through a private shard |

<Tip>
  Learn more in the [Confidential Treasuries section](/confidential-treasuries).
</Tip>

## Team Members and Roles

A treasury is composed of multiple members, and each member can be assigned multiple [roles and permissions](/governance/members-and-roles). There are three main roles in a treasury:

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Governance" icon="landmark" href="/governance/members-and-roles#governance" cta="View permissions" arrow>
    Manages team members and configuration, without handling day-to-day fund requests
  </Card>

  <Card title="Requestor" icon="user-plus" href="/governance/members-and-roles#requestor" cta="View permissions" arrow>
    Creates fund-related requests, such as payments and swaps, with no voting power
  </Card>

  <Card title="Finance" icon="badge-dollar" href="/governance/members-and-roles#finance" cta="View permissions" arrow>
    Reviews and votes on fund-related requests, but cannot manage governance settings
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Check>
  Team members can be assigned multiple roles, for example a member can be both Governance and Finance, which means they can manage team members and also vote on proposals.
</Check>

## Governance and Proposals

In a treasury, nobody can make unilateral decisions regarding the management of funds or team members. Instead, each treasury defines a minimum number of members who need to agree before performing an action—this is called the voting threshold.

All actions—whether making a payment, swapping tokens, or managing team members—must be **proposed first**, and relevant team members can vote on them. If a proposal reaches the required voting threshold, the system executes it automatically.

This way, treasuries ensure that all decisions are made collectively, thus minimizing risks and maximizing security.

---

## Security Model

NEAR Business secures treasuries through three mechanisms:

1. **Multiple members** – actions require approvals from more than one wallet
2. **Roles** – limit what actions each wallet can perform
3. **Voting thresholds** – define how many approvals are required for an action to be executed

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="Do I need to deposit funds to use a treasury?">
  A treasury is a wallet on its own, so you would need to deposit funds into the treasury to be able to use them.

  Once the funds are in the treasury, they can be managed by the team according to the defined roles and voting rules.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who holds the funds I deposited?">
  The treasury funds are held by the treasury, which is a multisig wallet smart contract on NEAR.

  The treasury is controlled by your team members through governance. The NEAR Business team does not custody your keys or funds, nor does it have access to them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if NEAR Business is unavailable? How can I access my funds?">
  Your funds remain on-chain and under the treasury control. Team members can use alternative interfaces or command-line tooling to keep managing the treasury.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I import an existing treasury?">
  If you previously created a `.sputnikdao.near` DAO, sign in with the account that created it to access your treasury.

  NEAR Business does not currently offer a migration tool for other treasury types. Contact the NEAR Business team to discuss your specific case.
</Accordion>
