[Treasuries](/confidential-treasuries) can [send payments](/payments/single-payment) in all our [supported assets and chains](/what-is#chains--assets).

This guide explains how to access and withdraw a confidential payment received through a [near.com](https://near.com/) account and transfer it to an external wallet.

---

## Accessing and Withdrawing a Confidential Payment

Navigate to [near.com](https://near.com/) and log in with the **wallet where you received the payment**. near.com supports a wide range of wallets from multiple ecosystems.

![near.com Send screen](/assets/docs/confidential/near-com-send.webp)

Click **`Send`** and select the token, destination network, and recipient address, then confirm the transfer.

![Selecting a token and network for withdrawal](/assets/docs/confidential/near-com-send-solana.webp)

<Tip>
  You can swap the funds to any other asset supported by [near.com](https://near.com/) before withdrawing them. The same asset can also be sent across multiple networks, so you can choose the option that best fits your needs.
</Tip>

Once the transfer completes, you will see the funds available in your selected external wallet.

![Completed withdrawal in near.com](/assets/docs/confidential/near-com-send-complete.webp)
